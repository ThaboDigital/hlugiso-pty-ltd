## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 8
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 with corporate Deep Teal (`#064E3B`), Charcoal (`#1F2937`), and crisp White tokens
- **Iconography**: Lucide React
- **Brand Assets**: Extracted directly from official vector/high-res branding (`public/branding/hlugiso-logo.jpg`)
- **Metadata**: Semantic Schema.org `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD, Open Graph, Twitter cards, sitemap.xml, robots.txt

## 📂 Page Architecture

1. **Home** (`/#home`): Hero with multi-service visual mosaic, trust strip, about teaser, 8-service pillar grid, unified capability framework, corporate cleaning callout, sector cards, why choose us, and high-impact RFQ CTA.
2. **About Us** (`/#about`): Founding narrative (Est. 2019), executive directorship profile (Managing Director Thabo Makola), tender readiness overview.
3. **Core Services** (`/#services`): Filterable catalogue of all 8 service divisions with detailed capability breakdowns, suitable client categories, and quote triggers.
4. **Cleaning & Facility Services** (`/#cleaning`): Dedicated commercial division showcase detailing daily office maintenance, carpet steam extraction, upholstery sanitisation, ablution hygiene, meeting-room staging, and OHS compliance.
5. **Industries & Sectors** (`/#industries`): "Who We Serve" targeting Government & Public Sector, Corporate & Business, Community Organisations, Event & Hospitality, Construction & Infrastructure, and Private Clients.
6. **Projects & Capability** (`/#capability`): "Demonstrated Service Capability" highlighting logistics fleet, cold-chain trailers, audio rigs, and field execution readiness without speculative claims.
7. **Company Credentials** (`/#credentials`): Statutory verification page featuring the printable/downloadable **Tender-Ready Vendor Credential Sheet** for supply chain officers.
8. **Contact Us** (`/#contact`): Complete Tzaneen head office coordinates, phone, email, registration details, interactive RFQ form, and instant WhatsApp dispatch.
9. **Interactive Modals**:
   - Multi-step **Request a Quote Modal** with service pre-filling and WhatsApp routing.
   - **Service Detail Modal** with full operational scope and capability checklists.
